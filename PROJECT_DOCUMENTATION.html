Project Overview

IlyassFit is a comprehensive personal training and fitness coaching platform designed to showcase your services, connect with potential clients, and manage your business efficiently.

🎯 Project Goals

  • Create a professional online presence for your fitness coaching brand
  • Showcase client transformations and success stories
  • Display pricing plans for both face-to-face and online coaching
  • Provide an easy way for clients to contact you
  • Give you a powerful admin dashboard to manage everything

Mobile App (PWA)

Your admin dashboard can be installed as a native app on your phone or computer for quick access and push notifications.

📱 Install on Mobile (Android/iOS)

1 Open your admin dashboard in Chrome/Safari

2 Tap the menu button (⋮ or Share icon)

3 Select "Add to Home Screen" or "Install App"

4 The app will appear on your home screen

💻 Install on Desktop (Windows/Mac)

1 Open your admin dashboard in Chrome or Edge

2 Look for the install icon (⊕) in the address bar

3 Click "Install" when prompted

4 The app will open as a standalone window

🔔 Enable Notifications

  • When you first open the dashboard, allow notifications when prompted
  • You'll receive alerts when new client messages arrive
  • Click the notification to go directly to the Messages page
  • Notifications work even when the app is minimized
